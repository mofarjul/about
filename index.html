Can I see every online product in the showroom?+

No. Our online range is larger than the showroom selection. Call before travelling if you want to see a particular item.

Is parking available?+

Yes. Free customer parking is available on site.

Can the showroom team help me choose furniture?+

Yes. Bring your measurements, photographs or questions and our team can help you compare products, dimensions, finishes, delivery and assembly options.

Do I need an appointment?+

No appointment is required for a general visit. If you need to check a specific product before travelling, please call us first.
